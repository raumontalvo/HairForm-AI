# HairForm AI — Version 1.0 Vision

## Mission

HairForm AI is an educational platform that helps professional hairstylists understand **why** color, chemistry, and haircut decisions work—not just what formula to use.

Our goal is to become the everyday professional companion for hairstylists through AI, interactive simulations, and visual education.

- Consultation Notes

- Client Name
